    How it works

    What stays on your phone

    The Lounge has no server of its own. Everything personal is stored and analyzed on your phone. Only film and weather lookups go out, plus the stylist chat if you turn it on.

    Stays on the phone Leaves the phone (no personal data) Only if she adds an API key
    If she…What happens
    adds a clothes photoThe color is read on the phone and the photo is saved in the browser's local database. It is not uploaded.
    scans a selfieThe face model runs on the phone. Only the result (face shape, undertone, eye color) is kept; the selfie itself is not saved.
    asks the stylist with a photoThat photo and her question are sent to Claude for the answer. The chat text is kept on the phone; the photo is not.
    uses "Tag with AI"Her closet photos are sent to Claude in batches of six to be named and categorized, then stay on the phone.
    opens the app on another phoneIt starts empty. Nothing syncs, because nothing is stored anywhere else.
